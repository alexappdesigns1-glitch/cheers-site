Cheers terms of use

Last updated: 12 October 2026

These are the terms for using Cheers, an app for logging the drinks you have and sharing them with your friends. By creating an account you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use Cheers.

Cheers is run by Alex Pearson, based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). You can reach us at hello@cheers-drinks.co.uk.

1. Who can use Cheers

You must be 18 or over, and old enough to drink alcohol legally where you live. You must give your real birthday when you sign up. We may close any account we believe belongs to someone under 18.

2. Your account

  • Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Give accurate details, and don't pretend to be someone else.
  • One person, one account. Don't create accounts by automated means.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings, then Account Details, then Delete Account.

3. What you post

What you post is yours: your photos, ratings, notes and comments. By posting it, you give us permission to store it and show it to your friends on Cheers, which is what the app is for. That permission ends when you delete the post or your account.

You're responsible for what you post. Only post photos and words you have the right to share.

4. Rules for everyone

Don't use Cheers to:

  • harass, bully, threaten or abuse anyone

  • post anything that encourages under-age drinking, drink-driving, or dangerous or excessive drinking
  • post someone else's private information, or photos of people who wouldn't want them shared

  • sell alcohol, or arrange its sale
  • try to break, overload or get around the app's limits and protections, or collect other people's information from it

5. Reporting, blocking and what we do about it

You can report a person, a post or a comment in the app, and you can block anyone. We look at reports and aim to act on them within 24 hours.

If someone breaks these terms, we may remove what they posted, and suspend or close their account. For serious cases we may do so without warning. If you think we've got a decision wrong, email hello@cheers-drinks.co.uk.

6. The details you log

Details such as a drink's strength (ABV) are there for your own record. Don't rely on Cheers for anything where the exact figure matters, such as deciding whether you're safe to drive. If in doubt, check the label.

7. Drinking responsibly

Cheers is a diary and a way to share what you enjoy. It isn't health advice, and it doesn't encourage you to drink more. If you're worried about your drinking, Drinkaware (drinkaware.co.uk) offers free advice, and your GP can help.
