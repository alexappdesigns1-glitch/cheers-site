You can report a person, a post or a comment in the app, and you can block anyone. We look at reports and aim to act on them within 24 hours.

If someone breaks these terms, we may remove what they posted, and suspend or close their account. For serious cases we may do so without warning. If you think we've got a decision wrong, email hello@cheers-drinks.co.uk.

6. The AI label reader

Cheers can read a drink's label from a photo and fill in its name and type for you. It can get things wrong. Nothing is saved until you've checked it and confirmed.

Details such as a drink's strength (ABV) are there for your own record. Don't rely on Cheers for anything where the exact figure matters, such as deciding whether you're safe to drive. If in doubt, check the label.

There's a daily limit on label reads per person.

Cheers is a diary and a way to share what you enjoy. It isn't health advice, and it doesn't encourage you to drink more. If you're worried about your drinking, Drinkaware (drinkaware.co.uk) offers free advice, and your GP can help.
