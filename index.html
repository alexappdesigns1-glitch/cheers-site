Rate your drinks.
Share them with friends.

Take a photo of your drink, give it a rating, and see what your friends are having.

How it works

  1. 1

    Take a photo

    Point your phone at the bottle, can or glass.

  2. 2

    Rate it

    Name it, give it a score out of 10, and add a caption or a place if you like.

  3. 3

    Post it

    Your friends see it on their Home page.

Better with friends

See what your friends rate

Home shows the drinks your friends have rated, with their photo and score.

Cheers the good ones

Tap Cheers, or double-tap the photo, on a drink you like the look of.

Have your say

Leave a comment, reply to someone else’s, or give one a like.

Your top shelf

Your best, at a glance

Your profile keeps your top rated drinks at the top, with everything you’ve rated underneath.

Yours to control

Read the privacy policy
  • Only your friends can see the drinks you rate.
  • No adverts, and your information is never sold.
  • Cheers doesn’t work out how much you drink.
  • Delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.
